ClaimsIQ Insurance Policy Manual

Motor Insurance Policy

Policy Number Format:
POLXXXXXX

Coverage:

1. Rear-end collision
Covered

2. Flood damage
Covered only if Comprehensive Policy is active.

3. Vehicle Theft
Covered after FIR verification.

4. Fire Damage
Covered

5. Engine Failure
Not Covered

Fraud Rules

A fraud score above 70 requires manual investigation.

Claims above $15,000 require supervisor approval.

Police report is mandatory for:

- Theft
- Major Accident
- Fire

Deductible

Motor Policy

First $500 paid by customer.
